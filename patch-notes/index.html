Patch notes

Career mode, realistic players and real football

The biggest soccer update yet.

  • Career mode (Windows, Mac and Linux): create your player with the player creator (face, skin tone, hair, beard, build, boots, position and attributes), sign for one of 12 clubs or build your own club with the team creator.
  • Play a full league season: fixtures, a live table, the Golden Boot race, training with skill points, contracts, transfer offers and end-of-season awards.
  • Everyone has an opinion: fans, the media, your manager, team-mates and rival fans react to how you play and what you say in press conferences. Clubs track your form and bid for you; commit to a club or push for a move and the internet lets you know about it.
  • A fake social media feed: club accounts, news, stats, pundits, transfer insiders, team-mates and fans posting after every match.
  • Realistic human players with shoulders, faces, kits with names and numbers, socks and boots. Turn them on for every mode in Settings > Character models.
  • Real football rules in career: a real-scale stadium (7.5 m goals, real markings, crowd, stands), 11 v 11 with formations and keepers, fouls, free kicks, penalties, yellow and red cards, and draws.
  • Dribbling: Q does a stepover or roulette, F flicks the ball up for a volley (also in Pro Soccer). Pro Soccer now has fouls and cards too.
  • Camera modes: Pro (behind you) or Broadcast (high TV view). Press V or Select to switch.

Career Mode (PC)

Your player, your club, your story. Windows, Mac and Linux only.

  • Create your player: name, position, stronger foot, shirt number, skin tone, hair, facial hair, build and boots. Bring over your Storm Royale skin, or create your own club.
  • Realistic human players with shoulders, faces and proper kits (names and numbers on the back). Switch every mode to realistic players in Settings.
  • Real-scale stadium: proper goals and ball, penalty areas, a packed crowd, sponsor boards and 11 v 11 football with keepers, a back line and offsides.
  • Real rules: fouls, free kicks, penalties, yellow and red cards (get sent off and the match plays out without you).
  • Pro camera (behind you) or Broadcast camera (TV view): press V or Select to switch.
  • Dribbling: Q for a stepover or roulette, F to flick the ball up.
  • A social feed where fans, pundits, rivals and team-mates react to everything. Press conferences, contract offers and transfer interest, with fans complaining when you commit or push for a move.
  • League table, fixtures, Golden Boot race, training, transfers and a trophy cabinet.

Storm Royale Mobile

A second, lightweight version of the game for iPads, phones and low-end PCs.

  • Storm Royale Mobile at /mobile/: loads in seconds, adjusts its resolution to stay smooth, and installs to your home screen.
  • 3v3 Soccer against bots with dribbling, volleys, headers, lobs, boost and the curving aim arrow.
  • Shot assist: shots curve toward the goal automatically. It is on by default in Mobile and the Android app, and you can switch it on in Settings on PC.
  • Battle Royale Lite: 12 players, a storm bus, a glider you open and close with Jump (land without it and you take fall damage), 5 weapons in 4 rarities, medkits and shields.
  • Controls built for iPad: a floating stick, drag anywhere to look, big buttons and aim assist. Keyboard, mouse and controllers work too.

Season 2.5: Replays, VAR, clans and play anywhere

Watch it back, argue with VAR, and play with anyone from any browser.

  • Replays: every goal, elimination and win is saved automatically. Press F8 (or Pause > Start recording) to record anything, and watch it all in REPLAYS with slow motion and 5 cameras.
  • Instant replays after goals, and a killcam on the death screen.
  • Offsides with a linesman flag, free kicks and VAR reviews that replay the pass with the offside line drawn (Pro Soccer and Ranked).
  • Passing: E for a ground pass to the team-mate you're facing, add Sprint for a through ball, add Aim for a lofted pass.
  • Volleys and headers with new animations: jump and kick a ball in the air. Long shots dip under the bar, with an aiming arc while you charge.
  • Six goal celebrations: Knee Slide, Sky Jump, Airplane, Heart Hands, Calm Down and Take a Bow.
  • Clans: create or join a team with a tag, roster, invite code, shared stats and a top-clans board.
  • Play online from any browser: Quick Play, host a room with a 5-letter code, or join a friend - no server setup.
  • Google Sites embeds: the mouse now works even where the page blocks mouse lock.

Season 2: Rivers, soccer playlists and friends

A big update across every mode.

  • Ball Arena playlists: Rocket Soccer, Pro Soccer (up to 5v5, stamina, no car), Power Soccer (pick 2 of 10 abilities), Golden Goal and Ranked.
  • Dribbling: the ball stays at your feet while you run until you kick it or get tackled. Bots pass, shoot and tackle.
  • Ranked ladder from Bronze to Grand Champion with rank points, streaks and promotions.
  • Better rocket car: powerslides that build boost, supersonic, double jumps, flips and boosted aerials.
  • Capture the Flag mode.
  • Rivers and lakes on the island with animated water and swimming.
  • Skydiving: the glider no longer opens by itself. Space opens and closes it, and landing without it hurts.
  • Accounts: usernames and passwords, friends, online status and Join party.
  • Fullscreen button (F11).

Ball Arena: set pieces

The ball can go out now, and play restarts properly.

  • New run-off strip past the lines, with corner flags.
  • Ball fully over a touchline: play stops for a throw-in to the other team.
  • Over the goal line: a corner kick or goal kick, depending on who touched it last.
  • Opponents must stay 5 m away. Aim and hold kick to throw or kick, or it goes automatically after 8 seconds.
  • Works online too. Banners no longer lag behind the kickoff countdown.

Season 1: Launch

Storm Royale is live in your browser.

  • Modes: Battle Royale, Tech Battle, Ball Arena, Practice Range and Online.
  • 101 Tech rules with a contradiction solver.
  • 158 items: weapons, abilities, skins with four styles, pets, gliders and pickaxes.
  • Automatic switching between keyboard/mouse, controller and touch, with aim assist.
  • Daily shop deals, quests and a 40-level season pass.
