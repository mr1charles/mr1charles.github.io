Ball control

  • Run into a loose, low ball to take it. It stays glued to your feet while you run and turn.
  • Hold kick to charge; release to pass or shoot. Longer holds hit harder.
  • Opponents who get close can tackle it away. Keep your body between them and the ball to shield it.
  • A ball that fully crosses a line is out: throw-in, corner kick or goal kick.
  • E passes to the team-mate you face: add Sprint for a through ball, add Aim for a lofted pass.
  • Jump into a ball in the air and kick for a volley, or meet it with your head for a header. An aiming arc shows where long shots will go.
  • Pro Soccer and Ranked use offsides: the linesman flags them, and goals from offside positions go to VAR.

Power Soccer abilities

Pick any two before a Power Soccer match. They go on Q and F.

Bicycle Kick

8s cooldown

Leap and flip; volleys any ball above or near you toward your aim.

Banana Shot

9s cooldown

A curling kick that bends around defenders and swings back on target.

Force Wall

18s cooldown

Raise a 7 m energy wall in front of you for 4 seconds. It blocks the ball.

Time Stop

18s cooldown

Freeze the ball in the air for 1.2 seconds. Perfect for setting up a volley.

Magnet Boots

16s cooldown

Pull the ball to your feet from up to 24 m away.

Power Shot

10s cooldown

Blast the ball flat and fast at full power. If it's out of reach, your next kick becomes the power shot.

Shockwave

15s cooldown

Knock nearby opponents away and stun them for a moment.

Speed Burst

12s cooldown

Three seconds of +60% speed. Keeps the ball at your feet.

Rocket Jump

7s cooldown

Launch high into the air to meet lofted balls.

Blink

14s cooldown

Teleport right behind the ball, lined up with the enemy goal.
