Game modes

Ball Arena

5 playlists - ranked, pro, powers

Soccer with rocket cars: Rocket, Pro (up to 5v5), Power Soccer abilities, Golden Goal and Ranked. Dribble, tackle, bicycle-kick.

Battle Royale

24 fighters - 1 winner

Drop onto the island, loot chests, build and outlast 23 rivals as the storm closes in.

Career

Your player - your club - PC only

Create your player (or your own club), sign for a team and play a full league season: table, transfers, training, skill moves and a social media feed that reacts to everything you do.

Capture the Flag

8v8 - first to 3 captures

Two teams, two bases, respawns on. Steal the enemy flag, carry it home and defend your own.

Practice Range

No storm - test everything

Free roam with training dummies and plenty of loot. Try any weapon, power or ability you own.

Tech Battle

Chaos rules - stack them up

Every time the storm closes a new Tech rule changes the game for everyone. 100+ rules, they never leave.
